COMMERCIAL PROPERTY — FLOOD AND SPECIAL PERILS INSURANCE POLICY

Insurer: Amani General Insurance Company Limited
Policy Number: AGI/FLD/2025/02116
Insured: Kariobangi Cold Storage & Logistics Limited
Risk Address: Plot 7, Outer Ring Road, Kariobangi South, Nairobi, Kenya
Occupancy/Use: Cold storage warehouse and distribution depot (foodstuffs, packaging, refrigerated plant)
Period of Insurance: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026
Hazard context (underwriting note): Risk sits within Nairobi Eastlands pluvial-flood corridors
identified in county hotspot lists (Kariobangi / Dandora / Njiru drainage catchment). Cover is
for pluvial and fluvial flood as defined below; catastrophe modelling for the book uses
susceptibility scores and return-period tiers consistent with Kenya Re Team A Nairobi flood desk.

SECTION A: PROPERTY INSURED

Item 1 - Buildings (cold rooms, loading bays, administration block, boundary walls)
  Sum Insured: KES 160,000,000

Item 2 - Plant and Machinery (refrigeration plant, generators, racking, material handling)
  Sum Insured: KES 95,000,000

Item 3 - Stock (frozen and chilled foodstuffs, packaging materials)
  Sum Insured: KES 125,000,000

Total Sum Insured: KES 380,000,000

SECTION B: PERILS COVERED

This Policy covers loss or damage to the Property Insured caused by:
  1. Flood — inundation of normally dry land by water from intense rainfall (pluvial),
     overflow of natural or artificial watercourses, or failure/overflow of public
     drainage within Nairobi County, including surface-water ponding
  2. Storm and tempest
  3. Escape of water from tanks, apparatus, or pipes (excluding gradual seepage)
  4. Fire (limited extension — see Section C)
  5. Lightning
  6. Impact by vehicles
  7. Riot, strike and malicious damage

SECTION C: EXCLUSIONS

This Policy does not cover:
  1. Loss or damage caused by the Insured's own wilful act or gross negligence.
  2. Loss or damage arising from war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, civil war, or
     terrorism, unless specifically bought back by endorsement.
  3. Loss or damage where the Insured has failed to maintain the flood-mitigation
     measures declared at inception (see Warranties 1 and 2 below).
  4. Consequential loss, including loss of profits, business interruption, spoilage
     from refrigeration failure unless caused by an insured peril interrupting power
     for more than 12 continuous hours, or loss of market, unless separately insured.
  5. Gradual damp, mould, or rising damp not attributable to a sudden flood event.
  6. Loss or damage occurring while the premises are left unoccupied for a continuous
     period exceeding 30 days, unless declared and agreed by the Insurer in writing.
  7. Fines, penalties, or punitive damages of any kind.
  8. Loss discovered more than 30 days after the Insured first became aware, or ought
     reasonably to have become aware, of circumstances giving rise to a claim, unless
     late notification is excused in writing by the Insurer.
  9. Damage to landscaping, unpaved yards, or temporary structures unless caused by
     an insured flood event and specifically scheduled.

SECTION D: WARRANTIES

Warranty 1 (Drainage and Site Flood Defence Warranty): It is warranted that the
Insured shall, at all times during the currency of this Policy:
  (a) keep site drainage channels, culvert inlets, and the connection to the public
      storm-water system clear of silt, solid waste, and debris, with documented
      clearance at least once every 90 days during the long rains (March–May) and
      short rains (October–December);
  (b) maintain any declared sandbag stores, temporary barriers, or bund walls in
      usable condition before forecast heavy-rainfall alerts issued by the Kenya
      Meteorological Department for Nairobi County;
  (c) not obstruct or permanently alter drainage paths shown on the risk survey
      without prior written agreement of the Insurer.
Breach of this Warranty, if material to the cause or extent of the loss, entitles the
Insurer to decline or reduce indemnity in respect of the affected claim.

Warranty 2 (Elevated Storage / Flood-Mark Warranty): It is warranted that stock and
critical plant shall be stored or installed with the lowest storage tier or plant base
not less than 0.45 metres above the finished floor level of the main cold-room hall
(the "declared flood mark"), except for goods in active loading/unloading for not more
than 48 continuous hours. Breach of this Warranty, if material to the extent of stock
or plant loss, entitles the Insurer to decline or reduce indemnity for the affected items.

SECTION E: EXCESS/DEDUCTIBLE

Each and every flood claim: KES 3,000,000, or 2.5% of the claim amount, whichever is greater.
Other insured perils: KES 2,000,000, or 2% of the claim amount, whichever is greater.

SECTION F: CONDITIONS PRECEDENT TO LIABILITY

  1. The Insured shall notify the Insurer within 14 days of any occurrence likely to
     give rise to a claim.
  2. The Insured shall furnish all information and documentary evidence reasonably
     required by the Insurer, including invoices, stock records, rainfall/flood
     photographs, and drainage-maintenance logs.
  3. The Insured shall take all reasonable steps to minimise or avert loss, including
     moving stock above the flood mark where safe to do so when flood warnings are
     issued for Eastlands / Outer Ring Road corridors.
  4. No admission, offer, promise, or payment shall be made by or on behalf of the
     Insured without the written consent of the Insurer.
