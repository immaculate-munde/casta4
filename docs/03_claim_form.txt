AMANI GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED
CLAIM FORM - FLOOD AND SPECIAL PERILS

Claim Reference: AGI/CLM/2026/2048
Policy Number: AGI/FLD/2025/02116
Insured: Kariobangi Cold Storage & Logistics Limited
Risk Address: Plot 7, Outer Ring Road, Kariobangi South, Nairobi, Kenya

1. DETAILS OF LOSS

Date of Loss: 14–15 April 2026 (overnight into early morning)
Time of Loss: Inundation first observed approximately 23:10 hrs on 14 April 2026;
peak water depth inside the main cold-room hall approximately 01:40 hrs on 15 April 2026
Date Reported to Insurer: 15 April 2026
Cause of Loss (as understood by Insured): Pluvial / urban flood following intense
rainfall over Nairobi Eastlands. Surface water entered from Outer Ring Road and the
blocked roadside drain adjoining the loading bay; water ponded across the yard and
entered the cold-room hall through loading-bay doors. Consistent with Kariobangi /
Dandora drainage-corridor flooding reported across neighbouring premises the same night.

2. DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT

The night supervisor, Ms. Amina Otieno, reported water rising across the yard at
approximately 23:10 hrs and raised the internal alarm. Staff attempted to move
palletised stock onto higher racking and to sandbag the loading-bay doors, but water
entered faster than barriers could be placed. Peak depth inside the main hall was
estimated at 35–40 cm above finished floor level. Floodwater began to recede after
04:30 hrs on 15 April 2026 as rainfall eased and the county storm-water system
partially cleared. No fatalities were reported. Refrigeration plant shorted when
water reached motor bases in the plant room; backup generator was started but could
not restore full cold-chain capacity until electrical isolation was completed.

3. ITEMS/PROPERTY AFFECTED

  - Main cold-room hall: floodwater damage to lower racking, flooring, and wall panels
  - Loading bay and yard: silt, debris, and damaged roller-door seals
  - Stock on ground-level and first racking tiers: substantial spoilage / contamination
  - Refrigeration plant motors and switchgear at base level: water damage
  - Administration block: minor water ingress at entrance lobby only
  - Upper-tier racked stock (above declared flood mark): largely unaffected, still
    being temperature-audited for cold-chain breach duration
